Data ownership: 
In a world with an all encompassing intelligence (AGI as may call it), you would not have your bank data in banking company, your health data in an healthcare company, your online social activity in a social network company, etc. You can image either the owner of the AGI holding all of this data to provide you a single point service, or instead you owning all of your data and the AGI accessing it to provide you the same service. The leverage would be very different if the AGI owner held your data than if you did.

GDPR is great, we need to continue building on it
